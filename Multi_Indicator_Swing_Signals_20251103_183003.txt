   → Short-term momentum is stronger than long-term
   → Institutional and retail traders both buying

   → Compression leads to expansion

✅ Volume Spike: Current volume > 1.5x average volume

✅ RSI Filter: RSI between 30-70
   → Not overbought (avoiding false breakouts)

🔴 BEARISH SIGNAL EXPLANATION:

   → Indicates the trend has shifted downward
   → Supertrend acts as dynamic resistance

❌ Guppy EMAs Bearish: Short EMAs (3,5,8,10,12,15) below Long EMAs (30,35,40,45,50,60)
   → Short-term momentum is weaker than long-term
   → Both retail and institutional pressure selling

   → Low volatility compression before potential breakdown
   → Setup for significant downward move

❌ Volume Spike: Current volume > 1.5x average volume

   → Distribution by large players

❌ RSI Filter: RSI between 30-70
